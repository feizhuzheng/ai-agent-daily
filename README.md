# China AI Radar 🛰️

A **daily-updated** tracker of the Chinese AI scene — model releases, open-source weight drops, and funding rounds — curated in public.

> Updated automatically every day. If it helps you, a ⭐ is appreciated.

## What's tracked
- 🚀 **Model / product releases** from Chinese AI labs (Kimi, DeepSeek, Qwen, MiniMax, Zhipu, StepFun, Hunyuan, …)
- 🔓 **Open-weight drops** (which labs open-sourced what, under which license)
- 💰 **Funding & IPO** milestones

## Files
- [`data/releases.md`](data/releases.md) — running log of releases (newest first)
- `weekly/` — a short weekly roundup every Saturday

## Why
The Chinese AI ecosystem moves weekly and the signal is scattered across Chinese/English sources. This repo pulls it into one place, in English, updated daily.
