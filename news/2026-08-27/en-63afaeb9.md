# Plaud Put a SIM Card in an Earbud Case So You Can Call Your Agents

来源 / Source: https://clauday.com/article/63afaeb9-0d64-4d8e-8c51-437d429e200c

Plaud, the AI recorder company, announced Plaud One, $249.99 earbuds whose charging case contains an eSIM with free cellular service in over 80 countries. The point of the SIM is not calls. It is that you can talk to Plaud's AI agents from the case, with no phone or laptop anywhere nearby. Preorders opened yesterday, shipping in Q4.

The hardware is built around capture. The earbuds record, the ring-box-sized case picks up voices within five meters, and together they log up to 25 hours of conversation per charge. Then the agent layer takes over: transcripts feed agents connected to Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, and Slack, which draft the follow-up email, build the document, update the notes. The pitch is that the meeting does not end with a recording, it ends with the work the meeting created, already done. A $200 AI credit bundle comes in the box.

Step back and the week composes itself into one picture. Anthropic shipped MHS so agents can operate lab equipment. Plaud shipped a pocket radio so your agents are reachable from anywhere your ears are. Hands in the factory, a phone number in your pocket, the agent stack is growing a body one appendage at a time, and it is mostly not the phone makers doing it.

The obvious tension is that a device designed to record 25 hours a day of ambient conversation is a consent problem wearing a product design, and Plaud's answer, the user stays in control, will get tested the first time someone's meeting ends up in someone else's Notion. But the direction is set. The microphone was the first agent peripheral; the SIM card makes it autonomous. Coverage at techcrunch.com.
