# free-claude-code Is the Grassroots Version of Routing Around the Frontier

来源 / Source: https://clauday.com/article/ecc6355d-5bc1-4051-a96b-b9fd4d7428f9

While the FT documents enterprises routing away from expensive models, a repo called free-claude-code is doing the same thing for individuals, and it is climbing GitHub trending fast, up over a thousand stars in a day toward 48k. The pitch is blunt: run Claude Code, Codex, Pi, OpenCode, and other coding agents with what it claims is 1.3 billion free tokens a month, by stitching together 49 different model providers.

It is not a crack and it does not bypass any paywall. It is a proxy layer that routes your requests across free and trial tiers from providers like NVIDIA NIM, Groq, and OpenRouter, using your own keys, with automatic fallback when one runs dry. It also cuts terminal output by up to 90 percent to save tokens, does voice transcription, and plugs into VS Code, JetBrains, Discord, and Telegram, plus local models through Ollama and LM Studio. The maintainers say they follow provider terms and pull integrations that stop being allowed, which is the honest framing for a tool that lives entirely on other people's free tiers.

The reason this belongs next to the Fable and Ox Alpha stories is that it is the same instinct expressed at the bottom of the market. Enterprises build routers to arbitrage token prices. Individuals install free-claude-code to do it by hand across every free tier they can find. Both are betting that the harness and the routing, not the specific model, are what you should own. When the frontier gets expensive, this is what the long tail does about it. Repo at github.com/Alishahryar1/free-claude-code.
