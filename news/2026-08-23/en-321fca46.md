# A Free Mystery Model Called Ox Alpha, and Everyone Thinks It's GLM

来源 / Source: https://clauday.com/article/321fca46-958d-442f-8390-79f97e9f6afb

An anonymous model named Ox Alpha showed up free on OpenRouter last Thursday, and the AI crowd has not stopped arguing about it since. The listing calls it a reasoning model built for coding, sustained agentic work, and production loads, with a 1M-token context window and text, image, and video input. It says only that it was made by a third-party provider who wants to stay anonymous during the preview. Free for about a week. That is the whole official story, which is exactly why it went viral.

The detective work is the fun part. Independent fingerprinting keeps pointing at Zhipu's GLM-5.3, probably the Flash variant. Early guesses ran through the usual Chinese labs, and a few people floated an unreleased Microsoft MAI, but no consensus. A viral 10-task DeepSWE run put it at 80 percent, ahead of Fable 5 at 65 and GPT-5.6-sol at 52, while a separate private benchmark rated it near two-generation-old models, so take the numbers with salt. Stripe's Patrick Collison called it very impressive, which is the kind of quote that keeps a stealth launch trending.

The timing is what matters. A frontier-tier model shows up free and anonymous in the same week the FT reports enterprises fleeing expensive flagship models for cheap ones. Whoever is behind Ox Alpha is running the free-preview playbook to buy attention and load-bearing usage while the frontier labs are getting squeezed on price. It is the clearest sign yet that in coding and agent work, good enough and free beats best and expensive for most of the market. Coverage at techcrunch.com and OpenRouter's model page.
