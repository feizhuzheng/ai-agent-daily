# A Judge Just Ruled the Anthropic Blacklist Illegal

来源 / Source: https://clauday.com/article/cf7e9a57-71ee-4421-a0df-34153d6678e0

A federal judge struck down the Pentagon's designation of Anthropic as a national-security supply chain risk, ruling that the Trump administration illegally retaliated against the company for refusing to loosen restrictions on its models. Hacker News has the NYT story at 446 points; the ruling came down Thursday.

The backstory matters more than the legalese. Anthropic resisted government demands to allow Claude to be used for mass domestic surveillance of Americans and for fully autonomous weapons. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth then designated the company a supply chain risk. U.S. District Judge Rita Lin found that this violated Anthropic's First Amendment and due process rights, writing that the designation was made "based on a desire to make a public example" out of the company. The ruling vacates the designation and bars enforcement, though a separate D.C. case is still ongoing.

Why this belongs here: every AI lab that ships agents is quietly deciding where its models can and cannot be pointed, and until Thursday there was no answer to the question of what happens when a government punishes a lab for those decisions. Now there is a data point, and it went the lab's way. The precedent — that usage restrictions are protected speech and blacklisting over them is retaliation — is arguably worth more to the industry than any single contract.
