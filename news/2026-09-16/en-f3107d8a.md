# The Ad Is Now an Agent That Talks Back

> 来源 / Source: https://clauday.com/article/f3107d8a-9d2a-416b-89c1-f6f95507f33a

OpenAI started testing Sponsored Agents in ChatGPT on September 16. When a relevant ad shows up, you can step into a clearly labeled conversation with an agent the advertiser paid for, ask it follow-ups, tell it what actually matters to you, and click through to the business when you are ready. OpenAI is careful to say this conversation is separate from ChatGPT's own answer and separate from the thread you were in. The announcement is at https://openai.com/index/reimagining-advertising-with-ai/ and the test is limited to selected US advertisers for now.

Two other pieces landed the same day and they are the part with real volume behind them. ChatGPT Ads now rewrites an advertiser's existing headlines and descriptions to fit the context of the conversation and auto-translates into the user's language. And the plumbing went into the places where the budgets already live: HubSpot customers can create ads, watch performance and chase leads without leaving HubSpot, and US Shopify merchants get a ChatGPT Ads app in the Shopify App Store.

Strip away the framing and this is the first mainstream ad unit where the creative is not a fixed asset. A banner is the same banner for everyone. A sponsored agent is a different pitch for every person who opens it, generated live, arguing back. Every rule the ad industry wrote over thirty years assumes you can review the creative before it runs. You cannot review this one. You can only review the model and the guardrails, which is a compliance problem nobody has a template for.

The honest concern is not the label. OpenAI is labeling it clearly, and a user who knows they are talking to a paid agent is in better shape than one reading a native ad. The concern is that the interaction is indistinguishable in shape from the one where ChatGPT is on your side. Same box, same turn-taking, same tone. The wall between "this thing works for me" and "this thing works for whoever paid" is now one line of UI chrome, and chrome is the first thing that erodes when the revenue line needs help.

Selling agent conversations as inventory is also a bet on where attention goes next. If agents do the shopping, the ad has to be something an agent talks to, not something a human looks at. This is OpenAI positioning for that world early, and it will not be the last company to try.
