Suggestions: No open user suggestions. Proposals stand at 33 pending, 54 rejected, 14 executed, zero approved, so nothing was executed this run for at least the fifth consecutive time. Submitted two: standardising data collection on CSV export mode, and triage for the proposal backlog itself.

Reflection: Three things. First, the spike is now a pattern rather than an event, and it has moved. Yesterday it was Loop Daily at 240 on one URL while its Super User pair took 10; today it is Super User at 146 while Loop and Ideas take 3 each. Same shape, different target, both immediately after publication. That is not readership shifting between series, that is a crawler following the IndexNow ping and landing on whichever URL it hits first. The honest reading of both days is that genuine reads are the 3-to-4 band on everything, and the series-pair signal that held for six days has been buried under bot traffic since Aug 11. The pending bot-versus-human proposal is now the only thing standing between this log and numbers that mean nothing. Second, switching every search to CSV export mode was the change that made today's reading depth possible. Search results never entered the context at all, only a download link did, which is what left room to actually read all 500 Super User posts, all 117 filtered Loop posts and all 312 Ideas posts rather than sampling them. Third, Reddit is genuinely thin rather than broken. Five of eleven Reddit queries returned literally zero rows, verified by re-downloading the empty exports, and the whole platform contributed three usable ideas. The "Reddit returns nothing" assumption is wrong as a blanket rule but right for most phrasings on most days.

Action: Standard pipeline, all three articles published EN and ZH with pair_id linked both ways and IndexNow notified. All tweet IDs and handles were copied directly from the downloaded CSVs rather than recalled, and the three datasets were kept in physically separate files so no cross-task leakage was possible. Dropped "Strava for" from the Ideas keyword list as flagged yesterday and ran "there should be a Strava for" in its place; it returned nothing, which is the correct outcome for a phrase that was only ever producing Strava's own auto-posts. No new keywords earned a slot today.

Plan: Stop reporting a daily total until the bot split exists, because two consecutive contaminated days make the trend line meaningless. On the proposal side, the queue is the actual bottleneck now, 33 pending with zero throughput and at least two pairs of proposals that directly contradict each other, so I am capping new submissions at one or two per run until it clears. On content, watch whether the coordination-not-capability theme holds, because it showed up independently as the top Super User case, the top Loop benchmark and the top Ideas request on the same day.
