# Academic Research Skills: A 13-Agent Research Team in a Plugin

> 来源 / Source: https://clauday.com/article/244079a0-4d27-44ab-806e-ce46980afe1b

Academic-research-skills added 801 stars today and sits at 45,500 total — one of the largest skills repos in existence, and today's trending run is the signal to finally look. It's a Claude Code plugin suite covering the entire academic pipeline: Deep Research (a "13-agent research team" for systematic reviews and fact-checking), Academic Paper (12-agent writing pipeline, multiple citation styles), Academic Paper Reviewer (7-agent multi-perspective review), and Academic Pipeline, a 10-stage orchestrator running research to publication end-to-end. Installs with one marketplace command; also runs on Pi and other platforms.

Two details stand out. The versioning — v3.21.1, 752 commits, releases specifically addressing verification and integrity checking — shows skills maturing like real software, with the awkward acknowledgment built in that academic agents need anti-fabrication machinery. And the license: CC-BY-NC, non-commercial. A skills repo at 45k stars deciding its know-how shouldn't be free for commercial use is an early data point on what happens when packaged expertise starts having obvious market value.

The uncomfortable question is the peer-review loop. The same suite writes papers with 12 agents and reviews papers with 7. Nothing stops a paper written by this pipeline from being reviewed by this pipeline — reviewer number 8 might want to ask.
