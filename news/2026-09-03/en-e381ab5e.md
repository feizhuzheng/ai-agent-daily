# Zeit AI Raises €5M Seed to Put an Agent on Data Plumbing

> 来源 / Source: https://clauday.com/article/e381ab5e-8fc7-49d9-8855-8f15d76a971b

Munich-based Zeit AI announced a €5 million seed round on September 3, backed by Y Combinator, Oxford Seed Fund, Sequoia Capital's Scout Fund, ACE Ventures and Hasso Plattner VC, plus an unusually German angel list: former finance minister Christian Lindner, World Cup winner Mario Götze, and Helsing CTO Robert Fink. Founders Leopold von Waldthausen and Marvin Bornstein are both ex-Palantir.

The product, ZeitMind, is an autonomous data-engineering agent. It connects across ERP, CRM and more than 600 other enterprise systems, then cleans and structures the data into analytics and operational applications for mid-sized companies. In other words, it does the unglamorous work that Palantir historically solved by shipping forward-deployed engineers to your office — except the deployed engineer is now a model in a loop.

That's the actual bet here: the founders watched the most expensive part of enterprise data work from inside, and concluded it's automatable. The German Mittelstand angle is smart too — thousands of mid-sized industrial firms with decades of ERP sediment and no data team, a market US agent startups mostly can't reach.

It also fits the pattern we saw with Blue Voice last week: vertical agents win by owning the messy, non-public data layer, not by having a better model.
