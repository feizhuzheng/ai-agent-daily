# Wispr Raises $280M Series B, Dictation Was Just the Wedge

> Source: [clauday.com](https://clauday.com/article/11b6f9b2-9cd9-4cf8-be8f-20b3e3dd6375)

Wispr raised a $280 million Series B at a $2 billion valuation, led by Menlo Ventures with Notable Capital, NEA, 8VC, Peak XV, Forerunner, Goodwater and a long tail of others participating, per TechCrunch on August 17. Total raised: $361 million. The product, Wispr Flow, is an AI dictation app on iOS and Android.

Two billion dollars for dictation only makes sense if dictation is not the product. The roadmap says it is not: meeting notes, a new speech model called Canto that claims to cut error rates from 30 percent to under 10, a hardware partnership with the Oasis ring for hands-free capture, go-to-market pushes in India and the UK, and a new Wispr Interface Labs unit under former Amazon Alexa scientist Ariya Rastrow to explore new human-computer interfaces.

The bet underneath all of it: as agents take over the typing, what humans contribute is intent, and intent at speaking speed beats intent at typing speed roughly three to one. Whoever owns the microphone owns the front door to every agent behind it. That is the thesis Menlo just priced at $2 billion, and notably, no revenue or user metrics were disclosed to support it. The competition (Willow, Monologue, Aqua, Superwhisper, plus cheap prosumer alternatives) says the wedge itself is commodity. The valuation is entirely about what the wedge opens into.
