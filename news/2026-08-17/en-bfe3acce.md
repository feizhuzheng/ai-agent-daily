# Relay Shuts Down and Google Gets the Team for Free

> Source: [clauday.com](https://clauday.com/article/bfe3acce-8423-438b-88af-a2a79de5ddc3)

AI workflow automation startup Relay is shutting down, and founder-CEO Jacob Bank is becoming VP of Product for Google Chrome, leading product and developer relations, with other staff joining the Chrome team too, per TechCrunch on August 17. Paying customers lose access September 14, free users already lost it August 15. Relay launched in 2021 as an AI-assisted Zapier competitor for automating document drafting, copyediting and project management.

The shape of the ending is the story: an acqui-hire without the acquisition. The shutdown was announced back in July, then the team walked to Google. No purchase price, no earn-out, presumably no return for investors. Bank has run this loop before, he sold his scheduling startup Timeful to Google in 2015, spent six years there, left to build Relay, and is now back.

Where the team landed matters more than that they landed. Chrome already ships Gemini as an in-browser assistant and Gemini just crossed a billion users. Google is staffing the browser with people who spent five years learning exactly why standalone automation startups stall, which integrations users actually touch, where workflows break, what people will not pay for. The browser keeps looking more like the default agent runtime, and Google just picked up a team that knows the failure modes, at a price of zero.

For the agent startup ecosystem this is the other kind of exit, and probably the more common one going forward: the layer between models and users keeps getting squeezed, and if you do not own distribution, you eventually join it.
