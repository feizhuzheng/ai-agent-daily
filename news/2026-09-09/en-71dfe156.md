# Cymphony Raises $25M Series A to Give Every Agent an HR File

> 来源 / Source: https://clauday.com/article/71dfe156-080c-4834-8d5e-c533f4bcc769

Cymphony came out of stealth with $30 million total, of which $25 million is a Series A co-led by Sequoia and SMBC's Fin Atlas Beyond Fund, valuing the New York and Tel Aviv company north of $100 million post-money. The rest is a previously undisclosed Sequoia seed, which is the detail worth noticing: Sequoia led twice, and Bogomil Balkansky pointed at enterprise traction, naming KKR and Syngenta as customers.

The product is a workforce graph. It maps every employee and every AI agent, what each can reach, and how they interact across corporate systems, then hands security teams that picture. Founders are CEO Shy Dekel with Idan Berkovits and Edi Gotlieb. The pitch is one observation dressed as a platform: agents now touch the same sensitive systems as staff, at machine speed, while inheriting none of the identity and access machinery built for humans. Your SSO knows who Dave is. It has no opinion about the twelve agents Dave spun up on Tuesday.

That gap is turning into a category fast. AIR took $50 million seed to police the skills and add-ons agents install (https://clauday.com/article/2ef19832-d348-476d-a0f1-e38b77ec60db); Cymphony sits one layer up, at identity and access rather than supply chain. Product Hunt's number two yesterday was Harden, a security layer for coding agents. Three companies, one week, all selling the same realization: the thing enterprises deployed as a productivity tool arrived on the network as an unbadged employee.

Watch what the graph is really for. Once you can enumerate every non-human identity and everything it touched, you have both the security product and the compliance artifact, and the second one is what gets budget in a regulated bank.
