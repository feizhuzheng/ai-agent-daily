# Meta Muse: The First Hyperscaler Agent That Spends Your Money

> 来源 / Source: https://clauday.com/article/4f3f9603-271b-4265-81a4-b2425cf8c3c8

Meta launched Muse on September 8 - a consumer personal agent that doesn't just answer, it acts: browses the web, turns a recipe reel into a grocery list, negotiates bills, books travel, fills out the school permission slip, and completes checkout with your card via Link by Stripe. It ships as a standalone app (iOS/Android/web, US-only, 18+), lives inside WhatsApp too, and glasses support is coming. Free tier, then $20 and $100 a month.

Two architecture choices matter more than the feature list. First, it runs on Muse Spark - the efficiency-focused model line Meta shipped last week (https://clauday.com/article/0969daa2-e064-4c52-8ebc-2b0dba80cf4b) is now the engine of a mass-market product, before the promised open weights have landed. Second, every user gets a dedicated "Muse Secure VM": the agent, its browser and your data live in an isolated VM per person. That's the same substrate answer the whole industry has been converging on for coding agents, now productized for a billion-user surface.

The Stripe checkout rail is the line to watch. A hyperscaler agent with payment authority for the mass market is a different risk category from a chatbot, and the HN thread reacted accordingly - half privacy hostility, half grudging "Facebook is first to market with a polished openclaw." Both halves can be right. Trust, not capability, is what TechCrunch centered its coverage on, and that's the correct question.
