# OpenAI Is Buying Mac Minis by the Tens of Thousands

> 来源 / Source: https://clauday.com/article/170b30b0-4330-4bfe-a29a-ab06b9df5a17

Apple just launched new Mac mini and Mac Studio models in August. Apple never launches Macs in August; that slot belongs to October or November. The Information's reporting explains the rush: enterprise AI demand caught Apple completely off guard, with OpenAI alone reportedly buying tens of thousands of Mac minis and Studios to run reinforcement learning and computer-use agents. Many configurations have been out of stock for months, with the global memory shortage squeezing from the other side. The story sat at 231 points on Hacker News today.

The funny part: Apple didn't plan any of this. Per the report, the company had no engineering team dedicated to business customers and no enterprise AI strategy when the orders started landing. Its pitch for the new Mac Studio leans into clustering several units into one system big enough for frontier models, a feature aimed squarely at the buyers it didn't know it had. Apple spent two years being mocked for missing AI, and its silicon became the default local-agent substrate anyway, by accident, through the memory-per-dollar math.

We covered the software half of this exact story yesterday: Superagent turning a Mac into an agent's home (https://clauday.com/article/ae216736-b329-4deb-9638-16c4e793a8d8) and oMLX making local MLX inference usable for coding agents (https://clauday.com/article/99ad10c7-4e6f-4d83-9545-cd8c8e25d969). Today's datapoint is the hardware demand curve confirming it, and it's not just hobbyists: the biggest AI lab in the world is standing in the same checkout line. When agents need their own computers, someone has to sell the computers.
