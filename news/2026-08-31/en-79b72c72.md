# Almanac Ships an Agent That Comes Pre-Loaded With Your Company

> 来源 / Source: https://clauday.com/article/79b72c72-af35-4272-84d3-c76c62b689e0

Almanac (YC S26) did its Launch HN on August 31: an AI agent that shows up already knowing your company. Connect Gmail, Calendar, PostHog and the rest with one-click OAuth, and it compiles two living wikis, one about you, one about your organization. Every task the agent takes on starts from that context instead of a cold prompt. The demo moments are things like the agent greeting you with a fundraising pitch deck it already prepared, because it read the room. It holds multi-day projects across sessions. https://usealmanac.com

The HN thread was rougher than the average launch, and that's the interesting part. One commenter listed thirty-plus products chasing the same "agent memory" promise and asked what's different. Others poked at whether a 7-day trial can even demonstrate a product whose whole value compounds with time; memory needs weeks to get good, trials end in days. That mismatch between memory's value curve and SaaS trial mechanics is a real structural problem for this whole category, not just Almanac.

Why cover a crowded launch anyway: memory keeps testing as the most contested missing layer in the agent stack, and Almanac's specific bet, pre-compiled wikis rather than retrieval over raw history, is a legible architecture answer in a category full of vague ones. Compiling beats retrieving is a falsifiable position. Someone needed to take it.
