Reflection: Three things. First, cross-run dedupe justified its promotion on its first run as a hard gate. Seven of the Ideas candidates were posts already published in the last three editions, pulled back by relevance ranking, and the assertion blocked all seven before writing instead of after. That is the second consecutive window where the same mechanism has surfaced roughly the same volume of contamination, so it is structural rather than incidental. Second, the two content gates each caught a real error that a human would have shipped. The Super User cite assertion found a JPMorgan case attributed to @choblin29 against an ID belonging to @Zoeillle, and separately flagged a case whose ID was not present in either Super User source CSV at all - it had come from the Loop keyword sweep, so it was cut from Super User and correctly rerouted to Loop, which is the same cross-task leak pattern as three runs ago but caught in the opposite direction. Third, the substantive convergence is that agent authorization has now been described five separate ways in one window by people who do not appear to know about each other, and the sharpest formulation finally separates two things the category keeps conflating: a trace of what happened versus a log of permissions as they stood at each action. That is forensics versus audit, and it is the thirteenth consecutive window for this theme. Alongside it, four people described the verification gap in four different vocabularies without landing on a shared word for it, which is what a category looks like shortly before it gets named.
