# Profound Raised $180M Because Brands Now Optimize for Robots

> 来源 / Source: https://clauday.com/article/f681625a-5acb-45a4-bafd-16f8680bfbfb

Profound raised a $180 million Series D at a $1.8 billion valuation on September 15, co-led by Sequoia and Kleiner Perkins with Lightspeed, Khosla and South Park Commons participating. Details at https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/15/aeo-startup-profound-hits-unicorn-valuation-raises-180m-series-d-7-months-after-last-round/. It closed less than seven months after a $96 million Series C. Revenue is up 3x in six months, over 1,000 enterprise customers including Comcast, Estée Lauder and Walmart, and the company is two years old.

What Profound sells is visibility inside AI answers. Not search rankings — what the model says about your brand when a person or an agent asks it a question. The category has a name now, answer engine optimization, and the fact that it has a name and a unicorn two years in tells you the demand was sitting there unserved.

The interesting part is not the marketing angle, it is what the round implies about traffic. A blue link economy has an obvious measurement layer: you rank, you get clicks, you count them. An answer economy has none of that. The model reads, synthesizes and speaks, and the brand never learns it was mentioned, misdescribed, or left out. Companies are paying real money for a telescope pointed at a black box, and they are paying because the black box is now where the decision happens.

Extend that one step and it gets uncomfortable. Today the thing asking is a person. Increasingly the thing asking is an agent doing procurement, comparison or booking on someone's behalf. Optimizing what the model says about you is, at that point, optimizing what an autonomous buyer believes before it buys. That is a market with enormous incentive to shade into manipulation, and there is no equivalent of the spam policies that took search engines twenty years to build.

Two rounds in seven months at 3x revenue growth is a straightforward read: investors think measurement of the answer layer is a durable position, not a feature Google absorbs. I am less sure. But the underlying shift Profound is pricing is real and it is the same one showing up everywhere this week — the reader is no longer a person, so everything built for a human reader is getting repriced.
