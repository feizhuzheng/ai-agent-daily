# Ideas Radar: 2026-09-13

> 来源 / Source: https://clauday.com/article/8c6a5bd2-c95b-4e94-a41f-7722e07d83ac

Two demand shapes carried this window. The first is the filter that has quietly moved in front of every funnel: when a person asks a model to shortlist three options, the brand never gets its moment to be liked, and what survives is explicit claims, specs written in text and prices, while atmosphere dies. Almost nobody is building for that filter. The second is the word actually, which turned out to be the highest-precision signal in the whole feed — a reverse image search for clothes that actually works, a reframing tool that actually follows the speaker instead of center-cropping. Underneath both, the agent authority gap hit its tenth consecutive window, this time with an enterprise number attached: 74 percent of enterprises think they can catch an agent failure before production, 19 percent have a gate that blocks. And the most underrated vein stayed where it was last window, in the trades — deep-hole concrete dust and retail opening-cost calculators both came back from different people, which in this feed counts for more than any single high-scoring post.

A funder publicly offering capital for a category nobody has cracked: an alternative, individualized and holistic K-12 learning experience. His reasoning came out of a homeschooling argument about socialization, where his answer was that his kids will socialize on jiu jitsu mats and soccer fields and in settings that foster competition, collaboration and good values, while the average school, even the best private ones, push kids toward bad decisions through the social structures they create. His claim is that whoever cracks this makes billions and has a massive societal impact. The product shape implied is not another tutoring app but a full replacement for the school as a social and educational container.

The most genuinely novel gap of the window, and it has nothing to do with rankings. Every purchase has a stage where a person narrows the world down to three options, and that stage is where most brand marketing does its work: photography, tone, the feel of the site, the thing that makes somebody decide they like these people. That stage is starting to happen without a person in it. When somebody asks a model to find the best option for a specific need and budget, the shortlist is assembled before any human looks at anything, so the brand never gets its moment to be liked — it gets read, compared on stated facts, and either included or left out. What survives is unglamorous: explicit claims, specifications written in text, prices, comparisons you make yourself in sentences on your own site. What does not survive is atmosphere, because nothing assembling a shortlist is moved by a photograph. The product opportunity is tooling for the filter in front of the funnel, and almost nobody is building for it.
Source: https://x.com/clicrank/status/2098064173762523552

An evidence layer for the internet, which is the most ambitious idea of the window and the one with the most carefully stated limits. Instead of labelling something true or false, it would show where a claim originated, what evidence supports it, what credible evidence contradicts it, whether multiple articles are independently confirming it or merely repeating the same source, whether the original information has become outdated, and what remains genuinely unknown. For an article claiming a new study proves a supplement improves memory, it would reveal the original paper, the number and type of subjects, whether it involved humans or animals, whether it was peer-reviewed, who funded it, and whether the headline exaggerates the findings. The features that matter are claim extraction, source genealogy tracing repeated stories back to their origin, time awareness warning when information is outdated, and AI-answer auditing sentence by sentence. The author names the hard part himself: evaluating evidence always involves judgment, simplified confidence scores could create false certainty, and much of the best research is behind paywalls.

Claude Code, Codex and Cursor appear across the agent-infrastructure ideas as the assumed substrate rather than as products anyone is asking for.

Shopify came up as a source of gaps rather than a solution: split fulfillment, multi-location inventory and the contradiction between its own documentation pages.

Zillow, Redfin and Domu were named together as the aggregation problem in housing, the same shape as the flight-seat watcher and the ADS-B scoreboard.

Notion Mail was named twice as a product people validated and then lost, which is the cleanest starting condition for a competitor.

ChatGPT Sites and GPT-6 Astra appear as the platform underneath at least one fully-specified service business.
