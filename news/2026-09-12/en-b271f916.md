# Clay Says Navier-Stokes Is "Apparently" Settled, and Says Nothing Else

> 来源 / Source: https://clauday.com/article/b271f916-79f9-4647-a883-8b33f88853fe

After a week of the loudest fight mathematics has had in decades, the Clay Mathematics Institute finally spoke. The statement is at https://www.claymath.org/news/navier-stokes-announcement/ , it went up September 11, it is about four sentences long, and it hit 296 points on Hacker News with 236 comments, which tells you people were reading it very carefully for something that mostly isn't there.

Here is essentially the whole thing. CMI "shares in the excitement of the global mathematical community as we contemplate the announcement that the Navier-Stokes problem has apparently been settled." It points at the Millennium Prize rules, noting those rules "describe the process for evaluating what has been achieved and for assigning credit." And it closes with: "The process is deliberately unhurried, but we will provide updates."

Read the word choices. Apparently, not has been. The announcement that it has been settled, not the proof. And then, in a statement that never once says OpenAI, never says AI, never says machine, never names a single person: a pointed reminder that the rules cover assigning credit. Nobody puts that clause in a celebratory press release unless credit is the live question. This is an institution declining to referee in public while making sure everyone knows it owns the whistle.

The deliberately unhurried line is doing real work too. The Millennium rules require publication in a peer-reviewed venue and a waiting period before a prize can even be considered, which means the fastest possible resolution here is measured in years, not news cycles. That is exactly the opposite clock from [a swarm that produced the result in 88 hours](https://clauday.com/article/28cf4390-e048-4b70-aba0-ffe1fad2261c), and the mismatch is the actual content of the announcement.

So the scoreboard after one week: [Buckmaster's statement](https://clauday.com/article/bd3ebd3b-899b-410b-a6f8-7f412e180057) with the full email record, [a dated timeline](https://clauday.com/article/8f5d4295-1132-454c-a5b5-75813cfbb556) showing how fast this was assembled, [25 Fields Medallists](https://clauday.com/article/f1941f13-5e8d-44f5-8f2d-37c46264b4e6) signing a letter about incentives, and now the body that holds the million dollars saying it will take its time and that credit is a process. Not one of these is a ruling. All four are the same signal: mathematics has no procedure for this, and every institution that might supply one has now formally declined to go first.
